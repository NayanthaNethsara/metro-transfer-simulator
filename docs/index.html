About / How to Use & Model Rules

All schedules, stop distances, dwell coefficients, and Markov traffic speeds are defined in docs/config.js. Per-leg speed scaling factors (alpha) are calibrated dynamically in your browser via bisection on seed or parameter reset.

  • Interactive inspection: Click any moving bus on the map, any row in the Connector table, or any item in the Transfer Log to jump directly to that Borella event.
  • Dwell formula: (12s + 2s * alight + 3s * board) / 60.
  • Traffic DTMC: 3-state peak/off-peak Markov chain calibrated via stationary distribution bisection (peak 06:00-09:00 and 17:00-20:00).
  • Objective J: 2.0 * transfer wait + 1.0 * on-board hold pax-min (60 min penalty if the last connector is missed).
